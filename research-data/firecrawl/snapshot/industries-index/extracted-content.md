# Where the stakes are highest, we build differently.

Different sectors carry different failure costs. A service outage in a hospital, a smart-city control room or a financial settlement flow is not a minor interruption — it is a business risk. Our solutions are designed around that reality.

## Government & Smart Cities

Fibre · IoT · Command centres

City-scale CCTV, IoT sensor networks and fibre backbones feeding multi-screen command centres, with incident-response dashboards that correlate events across feeds in real time.

## Banking & Financial Services

Network security · Blockchain ledgers

Hardened private networks carrying trade-finance and settlement ledgers, monitored by a managed SOC built to regulatory standards for financial-sector uptime and auditability.

## Healthcare

Critical uptime · Data protection

Critical facility ELV, nurse call, surveillance and patient-data protection engineered for hospitals and diagnostic centres where downtime has clinical consequences.

## Energy & Utilities

OT/IT convergence · SCADA security

Secure convergence between operational technology and IT networks, with SCADA-aware segmentation and monitoring for generation, transmission and distribution operators.

## Telecom Operators

Backbone · Edge data centres

Backbone and edge data centre builds for carriers extending coverage into new markets, engineered to interconnect cleanly with existing operator infrastructure.

## Hospitality

Guest Wi-Fi · Surveillance · IPTV

Guest-facing Wi-Fi, IPTV and in-room technology integrated with back-of-house surveillance and operations, so guest experience and security run on the same reliable network.
