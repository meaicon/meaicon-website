# Five disciplines,one accountable partner.

Most infrastructure programmes fail at the seams — the point where the network team hands off to the
security team, or the data centre design ignores what the ledger layer needs. Each MEAICON pillar is
delivered by a dedicated practice, engineered from the outset to interoperate rather than sit in a silo.


01

## Connectivity Solutions

Carrier-grade networks that keep sites, users and applications in sync — designed for the reality of last-mile delivery across MEA, not a single-country reference network.

— SD-WAN & MPLS architecture

— Enterprise Wi-Fi 6/6E

— Fibre backbone & last mile

— Unified communications

— 24/7 NOC monitoring

02

## Data Centre Solutions

Facilities engineered for uptime, density and controlled growth — from a single edge cabinet to a Tier IV core facility.

— Tier II–IV design & build

— Colocation & hybrid cloud

— Power, cooling & DCIM

— Disaster recovery sites

— Edge & micro data centres

— Managed detection & response

— Network & endpoint security

— Identity & access management

How an engagement runs

## Every build moves through the same four stages.

01

### Assess

Audit existing network, facility, security and data assets against operating requirements and regulatory context.

02

### Design

Architecture, vendor selection and budget locked together, so the design approved is the one priced and built.

03

### Build

Engineering, procurement and commissioning run under a single project office with weekly SLA-bound checkpoints.

04

### Operate

24/7 NOC and SOC teams take over monitoring, patching and incident response once the system goes live.
