## Source: Sonatus - What is OTA? Comprehensive Guide
URL: https://www.sonatus.com/blog/what-is-ota-a-comprehensive-guide/

### Key Concepts
- **OTA Definition**: Over-the-air (wireless) delivery of new software to vehicles
- **Use Cases**: Infotainment, navigation, telematics, ECU firmware, encryption keys
- **Automotive Leaders**: Tesla, BMW (OS 7/8), Mercedes MBUX - continuously scan for updates
- **Security Requirements**: Secure boot, signature verification, rollback protection
- **Fleet Management**: Centralized update orchestration across vehicle fleets
