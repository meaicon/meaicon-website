# MEDU Executive Summary — Mobile Sovereign Compute Platform

## Concept

Mission-ready GPU compute, wherever the mission goes. A single platform delivers high-density GPU compute, integrated power autonomy, and road-speed mobility — under a single contract with single-source accountability.

## Key Specs

- **240 kW** total IT capacity (single 40 ft container)
- **24 kW per rack** — highest in mobile class
- **+50°C certified operating temperature** — full N+1 at duty point
- **18+ days autonomous operation** — no grid, no resupply

## Platform Differentiators

1. **Modular cooling** — Patented hybrid heat-pipe + DX, +50°C rated, 8-bolt field swap
2. **Sliding-rail rack mobility** — Service access without compromise; racks translate fore and aft
3. **Truck stays coupled** — Compute and data roll within minutes of an operational shift
4. **Single-source accountability** — One contract, one warranty, one project manager, one SLA
5. **500 kW + 18 days autonomy** — Dedicated genset and ADR fuel — no grid, no resupply convoys
6. **Future-proof to GB200/post-Blackwell** — Chilled water infrastructure scales to direct-to-chip cooling
