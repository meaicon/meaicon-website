## Platform Differentiators

1. **Modular Cooling System** — Patented hybrid heat-pipe + DX technology, +50°C rated, 8-bolt field swap. Passive heat-pipe stage handles bulk thermal load; inverter compressor takes only the residual. 25–30% less energy per kW cooled.

2. **Sliding-Rail Rack Mobility** — Racks translate fore and aft along the container length, opening generous service aisles on either side. Full 360° rack access for cable management and component replacement. Reconfigurable layout adjusts rack spacing to deployment-specific airflow needs.

3. **Truck Stays Coupled** — Compute and data roll within minutes of an operational shift. The tractor stays coupled to the compute trailer at all times. Designed to move daily, not just ship once.

4. **Single-Source Accountability** — One contract, one warranty, one project manager, one SLA, one accountable signature on performance. Integration risk lives with the supplier, not the project team.

5. **Power Independence** — 500 kW dedicated genset and 10,000-gallon ADR-certified fuel tanker. No grid, no resupply convoys. Continuous full IT load at +50°C ambient.

6. **Future-Proof Cooling** — Chilled-water infrastructure scales to direct-to-chip cooling within the same container envelope. Supports current and next-generation accelerated computing.

## Modular Cooling Scaling

- **Day One**: 20 kW cooling capacity — Initial deployment with N+1 cooling, full mission capability from day one.
- **Year One**: +15 kW cooling capacity — Mission scope expands. Add three wall-mounted units through pre-cut apertures.
- **Steady State**: 35 kW cooling capacity — Full configuration with reserved expansion capacity for future growth.

## MEDU Architecture (Three Trailers)

1. **Tractor** — 540 hp, stays coupled to compute trailer at all times
2. **Compute Trailer** — 240 kW IT, permanent low-bed
3. **Power Trailer** — 500 kW genset, sound-attenuated, 50°C ambient rated, N+1 paralleled-ready
4. **Fuel Trailer** — 10,000-gallon diesel, ADR-certified tanker with integrated transfer pumps
