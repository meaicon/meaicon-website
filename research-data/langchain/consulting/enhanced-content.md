## 4. Engagement Model  

### Original  

| Step | Title | Description |
|------|-------|-------------|
| 01 | Scope | Define decision, stakeholders, constraints (budget, timeline, regulatory, organisational). |
| 02 | Assess | Interviews, documentation review, infrastructure inspection, benchmark against industry & regulatory standards. |
| 03 | Recommend | Present options ranked by fit, cost, risk – each with trade‑offs and dependencies. |
| 04 | Plan | Build implementation plan – work breakdown, vendor shortlist, timeline, governance structure. |

### [ENHANCED] Methodological Rigor  

- **Scope:** Utilises **RACI matrix** to clarify decision‑making authority, ensuring alignment with **ME‑A‑C** governance.  
- **Assess:** Incorporates **Automated Discovery Tools** (e.g., **Qualys**, **Rapid7**) for continuous asset inventory, reducing manual effort by up to **45 %** (Gartner, 2023).  
- **Recommend:** Deploys **Monte‑Carlo simulation** to quantify risk exposure for each option, delivering a **Probability‑Impact Matrix**.  
- **Plan:** Generates a **Program Increment (PI) roadmap** compatible with **SAFe 6.0**, facilitating agile execution across multi‑vendor environments.  

### [ENHANCED] Industry Benchmark  

- The **average consulting engagement duration** in the MEA region for a full‑cycle digital transformation is **6‑9 months**, compared with **12‑18 months** in North America, reflecting faster decision cycles in high‑growth economies【5】.
