## 2.  Connectivity Infrastructure in MEA  
[ENHANCED] **Submarine Cable Network**  
MEAICON’s backbone leverages a mix of terrestrial fiber and submarine cable systems that connect the MEA region to global internet hubs.  
- **Key Submarine Cables**:  
  - *Arab-Med (AM)* – connects the Gulf to the Mediterranean, passing through UAE, Saudi Arabia, and Egypt.  
  - *LION (Linking the Indian Ocean Network)* – links Oman and UAE to East Africa and India.  
  - *MEC (Middle East Cable)* – provides direct connectivity between Qatar, UAE, and the UAE’s offshore data centres.  
  - *East Africa Submarine Cable (EAS)* – links Kenya to the global network via the Indian Ocean.  

> **Capacity & Latency**  
> - Combined submarine capacity exceeds 100 Tbps, with average latency of < 50 ms between the UAE and key global markets (London, New York, Singapore).  
> - Terrestrial fiber routes provide 10 Gbps+ links to national backbones, supporting enterprise‑grade bandwidth requirements.  

> **Redundancy & Resilience**  
> - Dual‑path routing across multiple submarine and terrestrial links ensures 100 % path diversity.  
> - Automatic fail‑over mechanisms trigger within seconds of link degradation, maintaining service continuity.  

> **Industry Trend**  
> The MEA region has seen a 30 % CAGR in submarine cable deployment over the past five years, driven by data‑driven economies and cloud migration. (Source: GSMA, *Submarine Cables in the Middle East & Africa*, 2024)
