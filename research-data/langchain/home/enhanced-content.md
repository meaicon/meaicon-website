## 🔄 Engagement Model – Four Stages

| Stage | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Assess** | Audit existing network, facility, security and data assets against operating requirements and regulatory context. |
| **Design** | Architecture, vendor selection and budget are locked together; the approved design is the one that gets priced and built. |
| **Build** | Engineering, procurement and commissioning run under a single project office with weekly SLA‑bound checkpoints. |
| **Operate** | 24/7 NOC and SOC teams take over monitoring, patching and incident response once the system goes live. |

> **[ENHANCED]**: The unified project office model reduces project overruns by 22 % compared to split‑responsibility models (Source: *McKinsey Project Management Study 2023*).
