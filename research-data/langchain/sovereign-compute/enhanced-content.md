## Overview

### Infrastructure independence for sensitive workloads

MEAICON LLC FZ's sovereign compute practice addresses the growing demand from MEA governments and defence organisations for infrastructure that operates within national borders, under national jurisdiction, and independent of foreign cloud providers. We design and deploy GPU-accelerated compute platforms that keep training data, model weights, and inference outputs within controlled environments.

Our platforms are designed for operational environments where the public cloud is not an option — classified defence networks, government data centres subject to national sovereignty laws, and critical infrastructure with air-gapped security requirements. Each deployment is tailored to the jurisdiction's regulatory framework, whether that means UAE Federal Law on Data Protection, Saudi PDPL, or sector-specific defence and national security regulations.

We provide multiple capacity tiers — from 48 kW compact deployments suitable for field operations and tactical facilities, to 240 kW high-density configurations for centralised government AI training and inference centres. Each tier is engineered for the MEA region's power, cooling, and environmental realities.

## Capabilities

### What we deliver

**Data-Local GPU Clusters**
High-density GPU clusters configured for AI training and inference workloads — NVIDIA H100/A100/L40S configurations with InfiniBand fabric for distributed training. All data, models, and processing remain within the controlled jurisdiction. No data egress to foreign infrastructure.

**Power-Autonomous Operation**
Platforms designed for locations where grid power is unreliable or unavailable — generator-backed with UPS, solar-augmented configurations for remote sites, and fuel-cell options for extended autonomous operation. Power systems are sized for sustained GPU workload operation, not just IT baseline.

**Air-Gapped Security**
Network-isolated deployments for classified and ultra-sensitive workloads. We implement physical network segregation, TEMPEST hardening for electromagnetic emanations, and secure facility integration compliant with national security standards.

**Sovereign AI Training**
Full-stack AI training capability within sovereign infrastructure — data pipelines, training orchestration, distributed training across GPU nodes, model evaluation, and deployment. We implement MLflow and Kubeflow for training tracking and reproducibility within the sovereign environment.

**Jurisdictional Compliance**
Architecture and operations aligned with national data sovereignty requirements. We work with government legal and security teams to ensure platform design, data handling, access controls, and audit trails meet jurisdiction-specific mandates.

**Capacity Tiers**
- 48 kW compact: Tactical, field, or temporary deployments — 4-8 GPUs, ruggedised form factor
- 120 kW mid-density: Departmental or regional government facilities — 16-32 GPUs
- 240 kW high-density: Central AI training centres — 64+ GPUs with liquid cooling

## Delivery Approach

### How it works

**01 — Sovereignty Assessment**
We work with government security and legal teams to define sovereignty requirements — data classification, jurisdictional boundaries, access controls, audit requirements, and network isolation levels. This produces a sovereignty specification that governs all subsequent design decisions.

**02 — Architecture & Design**
We architect the sovereign compute platform — GPU cluster topology, storage architecture, networking (including air-gap design), power and cooling systems, and security hardening. Design is validated against the sovereignty specification and national security standards.

**03 — Build & Integrate**
We procure, integrate, and commission the platform within the controlled environment. Build includes GPU cluster installation, InfiniBand fabric configuration, training software stack deployment, and security validation including penetration testing of the sovereign environment.

**04 — Operate & Audit**
Ongoing operation, security monitoring, and compliance auditing of the sovereign compute platform. We provide capacity utilisation reporting, security audit logs, and periodic sovereignty compliance reviews to ensure the platform remains within jurisdictional boundaries.
