# Case Studies — MEAICON Results Across Banking & Government

## Overview
Selected MEAICON engagements across banking, government, healthcare, telecom, energy, retail, and hospitality in the Middle East and Africa.

A selection of engagements where MEAICON designed, built, and operated infrastructure that governments and enterprises across MEA rely on.

MEAICON engagements span banking and financial services, government and smart cities, healthcare, telecom, energy and utilities, retail, hospitality, maritime, and logistics — each one a multi-month or multi-year programme where we designed, built, and continue to operate infrastructure that organisations across the Middle East and Africa depend on daily. The case studies below represent a cross-section of this work, chosen to illustrate the breadth of our capabilities and the depth of our regional expertise.

What connects these engagements is not a single technology stack but a consistent methodology: rigorous assessment before architecture, proof-of-concept before scale, and managed services after handover. In every case, the client's data sovereignty, regulatory compliance, and operational continuity were non-negotiable constraints that shaped the solution — not afterthoughts bolted on at the end. The outcomes speak for themselves: measurable reductions in latency, cost, and risk, alongside demonstrable improvements in uptime, security posture, and user experience.

### Trade-finance blockchain ledger

A regional banking group needed a shared ledger for cross-border trade documents alongside a hardened security posture.

### Smart city infrastructure

A government agency needed integrated fibre, IoT, and a command centre for public infrastructure monitoring.

### Critical uptime programme

A healthcare provider needed zero-downtime network and data protection for critical care systems.

### Edge computing deployment

A telecom operator needed edge infrastructure to support low-latency services across multiple sites.

### SCADA security overhaul

An energy operator needed to secure OT/IT convergence across SCADA networks without disrupting operations.

### Omnichannel platform

A retail group needed an integrated network and payment security platform across physical and digital channels.

### Guest experience infrastructure

A hospitality group needed guest Wi-Fi, surveillance, and IPTV across multiple properties with centralised management.

### Fleet predictive maintenance

How edge AI with on-device neural processing delivers predictive fault detection and closed-loop maintenance automation for vehicle fleets.
