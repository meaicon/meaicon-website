# Post-Quantum Security

## MEAICON LLC FZ – Post-Quantum Security

**Positioning:** MEAICON LLC FZ leads the adoption of **Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC)** to future‑proof critical infrastructure against quantum computing threats.

## Why MEAICON

As quantum computers mature, classical asymmetric algorithms (RSA, ECC) become vulnerable. MEAICON proactively implements PQC algorithms to safeguard data confidentiality and integrity:

- **NIST‑Standardized PQC Algorithms** – Integration of Kyber (key encapsulation) and Dilithium (digital signatures) for forward secrecy.
- **Hybrid Encryption** – Classical + PQC cipher suites during transition period.
- **Quantum‑Resistant Key Management** – Safe rotation and distribution of PQC keys across edge nodes.
- **Threat‑Adjusted Risk Management** – Continuous monitoring of quantum‑computing capability evolution.
