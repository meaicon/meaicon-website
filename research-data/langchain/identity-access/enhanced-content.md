# Identity Access Management

## MEAICON LLC FZ – Identity Access Management (IAM)

**Positioning:** MEAICON LLC FZ delivers a **Unified Identity Access Management (IAM)** platform that combines strong authentication, role‑based access control (RBAC), and continuous authorization to protect critical infrastructure assets.

## Why MEAICON

Our IAM solution goes beyond password‑based login to embed **identity‑centric security** into every digital interaction:

- **Single Sign‑On (SSO)** – Seamless access across cloud, on‑premises, and SaaS applications.
- **Conditional Access** – Access decisions driven by device posture, location, and risk context.
- **Privileged Access Management (PAM)** – Just‑in‑time elevation of admin privileges with session recording.
- **Compliance‑Ready** – Built‑in controls for NESA, SAMA, and CITC regulatory requirements.

## Key Capabilities

| Capability | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| **SSO & Federation** | SAML/OIDC federation with enterprise identity providers (Okta, Azure AD, LDAP). |
| **RBAC & ABAC** | Role‑based and attribute‑based access policies with fine‑grained permissions. |
| **Just‑In‑Time (JIT) Privilege** | Temporary elevation of admin rights with automated revocation. |
| **Device Posture Checks** | Continuous assessment of endpoint health before granting access. |
| **Audit Trail & Compliance** | Immutable logs for SOC 2, ISO 27001, and MEA region audits. |

## Benefits

- **Reduced Attack Surface** – Eliminates credential sprawl and weak passwords.
- **Faster Provisioning** – Self‑service onboarding with pre‑approved access templates.
- **Regulatory Confidence** – Meets MEA‑specific compliance mandates out of the box.
- **Operational Efficiency** – Centralized governance reduces manual ticket cycles.

## MEAICON Differentiation

Unlike traditional IAM products, MEAICON embeds **identity‑aware security** directly into the network edge, creating a zero‑trust identity perimeter that scales with OT/IT convergence.
