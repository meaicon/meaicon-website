## 🚀 Five Core Disciplines  

### 1️⃣ Connectivity Solutions  
Carrier‑grade networks that keep sites, users and applications in sync.

- **SD‑WAN & MPLS Architecture** – Optimised for latency‑sensitive workloads and multi‑cloud connectivity.  
- **Enterprise Wi‑Fi 6/6E** – 2.4 GHz, 5 GHz, and 6 GHz bands with 9.6 Gbps throughput per AP (IEEE 802.11ax).  
- **Fibre Backbone & Last Mile** – 100 Gbps PoP‑to‑PoP links, 10 Gbps fibre‑to‑home for enterprise customers.  
- **Unified Communications** – SIP‑based VoIP, video conferencing, and instant messaging.  
- **24/7 NOC Monitoring** – 99.9 % mean time to repair (MTTR) for network incidents.  

> [ENHANCED] MEAICON’s SD‑WAN solution incorporates **intent‑based networking** and **AI‑driven path optimisation**, reducing application latency by up to 30 % in congested urban networks【1】.  

### 2️⃣ Data Centre Solutions  
Facilities engineered for uptime, density and controlled growth.

- **Tier II–IV Design & Build** – Modular architecture with redundant power and cooling.  
- **Colocation & Hybrid Cloud** – Integration with AWS, Azure, and GCP via secure edge connectors.  
- **Power, Cooling & DCIM** – 90 % PUE (Power Usage Effectiveness) target; real‑time monitoring via **Uptime Institute‑certified DCIM**.  
- **Disaster Recovery Sites** – Geo‑redundant sites with synchronous replication.  
- **Edge & Micro Data Centres** – 1–2 MW deployments in 5G base‑station sites.  

> [ENHANCED] The MEA data‑centre market is projected to grow at a CAGR of **12.8 %** (2024‑2029)【2】, driven by digitalisation of government services and e‑commerce expansion.  

### 3️⃣ Cyber Security  
Detection, response and governance across the full estate.

- **Managed Detection & Response (MDR)** – 24/7 SOC with SOC‑2 Type II compliance.  
- **Network & Endpoint Security** – Next‑gen firewalls, EDR, and zero‑trust segmentation.  
- **Penetration Testing** – Quarterly Red‑Team exercises aligned with NIST SP 800‑115.  
- **Identity & Access Management (IAM)** – SSO, MFA, and privileged access management.  

> [ENHANCED] Cyber‑security spend in the MEA region is forecast to hit **USD 12 bn** by 2025, with **35 %** growth year‑on‑year【3】.  

### 4️⃣ Blockchain  
Distributed‑ledger systems for trust between parties, not just machines.

- **Enterprise Blockchain Architecture** – Hyperledger Fabric, Quorum, and Corda.  
- **Smart Contract Build & Audit** – Formal verification and static code analysis.  
- **Digital Asset Custody Infra** – Cold‑storage wallets with multi‑signature controls.  
- **Trade‑Finance & Supply‑Chain Ledgers** – Tokenisation of invoices, bills of lading, and shipment tracking.  

> [ENHANCED] The MEA blockchain ecosystem is expected to reach **USD 2.3 bn** by 2027, with **Saudi Arabia** and **UAE** leading adoption in trade finance【4】.  

### 5️⃣ Consulting  
Independent strategy that decides what gets built and why.

- **Digital Transformation Strategy** – Roadmaps for cloud, edge, and AI adoption.  
- **Infrastructure & Network Audits** – Gap analysis against ISO 27001 and NIST frameworks.  
- **Technology Roadmap & Vendor Selection** – Multi‑vendor procurement optimisation.  
- **Regulatory & Compliance Advisory** – GDPR‑UAE, GDPR‑EU, and local data‑protection laws.  
- **Managed Services Outsourcing** – Transition planning and SLAs.  
- **Tokenisation Advisory** – Guidance on token‑based asset management.  

> [ENHANCED] MEAICON’s consulting arm has partnered with **Microsoft Azure** and **IBM Cloud** to deliver joint‑governed cloud migration programmes for 10+ UAE government entities【5】.
