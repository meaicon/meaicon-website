## Overview

### Distributed processing built for MEA operational realities

MEAICON LLC FZ's edge compute practice addresses the unique challenges of operating across the Middle East and Africa — extreme climate conditions, varied telecom infrastructure, distributed operational sites, and the need for autonomous processing when connectivity is intermittent.

Our edge architectures span three tiers: compact mobile nodes (48 kW) for tactical and temporary deployments, fixed edge sites for permanent infrastructure at industrial and government facilities, and cloud-edge topologies that integrate edge processing with centralised cloud platforms for unified management and analytics.

Each tier is designed with hardware appropriate to the environment — ruggedised enclosures for +50°C operation, IP-rated environmental protection for dust and moisture, and power systems that accommodate generator-backed or solar-augmented operation in locations where grid reliability is uncertain.


## Capabilities

### What we deliver

**Mobile Edge Nodes (48 kW)**
Compact, transportable compute platforms delivering 48 kW of processing capacity in a ruggedised form factor. Designed for rapid deployment to temporary sites, disaster response, tactical operations, and event-driven capacity needs. Each node includes integrated cooling, power management, and network connectivity.

**Fixed Edge Sites**
Permanent edge computing facilities at industrial sites, government installations, campus environments, and critical infrastructure locations. Designed for continuous operation with redundant power, environmental monitoring, and remote management capabilities compliant with DCIM standards.

**Cloud-Edge Integration**
Architecture that unifies distributed edge nodes with centralised cloud platforms for consistent management, data synchronisation, and analytics. We implement edge orchestration platforms that handle workload placement, data locality policies, and failover across the edge-to-cloud continuum.

**IoT Data Aggregation**
Edge-based data collection and pre-processing for IoT sensor networks — filtering, normalisation, and aggregation at the edge to reduce bandwidth requirements and enable local decision-making. We support protocols including MQTT, CoAP, and OPC UA for industrial environments.

**Ruggedised Hardware**
Hardware selection and integration for extreme environments — +50°C ambient operation, dust ingress protection (IP54–IP65), humidity resistance, and shock/vibration tolerance for mobile and maritime platforms. We partner with ruggedised hardware vendors certified for MEA operational conditions.

**Edge Networking**
Low-latency networking between edge nodes, field devices, and central platforms — including 5G edge integration, Wi-Fi 6E for high-density environments, and deterministic networking for time-sensitive applications using TSN (Time-Sensitive Networking).


## Delivery Approach

### How it works

**01 — Assess**
We evaluate operational sites, environmental conditions, connectivity availability, workload latency requirements, and data sovereignty constraints. The assessment produces a site suitability matrix and edge tier recommendation for each location.

**02 — Design**
We architect the edge topology — node placement, inter-node networking, cloud-edge integration, power and environmental systems, and workload distribution. Design includes failover strategies, data synchronisation policies, and security architectures for distributed processing.

**03 — Deploy**
We procure, integrate, and install edge hardware at operational sites. Deployment includes configuration of orchestration platforms, security hardening, connectivity testing, and operational validation against environmental and performance criteria.

**04 — Manage**
Ongoing monitoring, remote management, firmware updates, and capacity planning for distributed edge infrastructure. We provide centralised visibility into all edge nodes with automated alerting and predictive maintenance scheduling.
