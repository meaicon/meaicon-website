## 2. Technical Architecture  
### 2.1 SD‑WAN & MPLS  
- **MPLS** provides deterministic QoS, low latency, and secure site‑to‑site connectivity.  
- **SD‑WAN** overlays the MPLS core, enabling dynamic path selection, application‑aware routing, and cloud‑directed traffic.  
- **Hybrid model**: MPLS for critical latency‑sensitive workloads; SD‑WAN for cost‑effective, elastic connectivity to public clouds and edge sites.  

### 2.2 Enterprise Wi‑Fi 6/6E  
- **Wi‑Fi 6** (802.11ax) delivers up to 9.6 Gbps aggregate throughput, 4× higher density, and 5× lower latency compared to Wi‑Fi 5.  
- **Wi‑Fi 6E** extends to the 6 GHz band, offering 7× more non‑overlapping channels, reducing congestion in dense urban deployments.  
- **Security**: WPA3‑Enterprise, 802.1X authentication, and integrated threat detection via 802.11k/v.  

### 2.3 Fibre Backbone & Last‑Mile  
- **Sub‑marine & terrestrial fibre** interconnects major MEA hubs (Dubai, Riyadh, Cairo, Nairobi).  
- **Active‑optical last‑mile** (GPON/EPON) delivers 10 Gbps per cabinet, enabling edge‑to‑core scalability.  
- **Redundancy**: Dual‑path, cross‑connected ring topologies protect against fiber cuts and node failures.  

### 2.4 Unified Communications (UC)  
- **IP‑based voice, video, chat, and collaboration** integrated into the SD‑WAN fabric.  
- **Session Border Controllers (SBCs)** provide media encryption, DDoS mitigation, and regulatory compliance.  
- **Cloud‑first UC** (Teams, Zoom, Webex) is routed via SD‑WAN for optimal performance.  

### 2.5 24/7 NOC Monitoring  
- **Real‑time telemetry** (SNMP, NetFlow, sFlow, gRPC) feeds a SIEM for automated alerts.  
- **Proactive fault detection** (predictive analytics) reduces MTTR by up to 30 %.  
- **Service‑level dashboards** expose KPI metrics to enterprise stakeholders.
