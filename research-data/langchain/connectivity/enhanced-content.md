## Operating Model  

### Local Delivery, Centralised Operations  
**Original content**  
> Delivery teams are local — they understand the market, the language, and the regulatory environment. Operations are centralised in our 24/7 NOC/SOC, so monitoring and incident response are consistent across all markets.  

**[ENHANCED] Technical Depth**  
- **SD‑WAN & MPLS**: All deployments leverage hybrid SD‑WAN over MPLS, allowing dynamic path selection and fail‑over across fibre and satellite backhaul.  
- **Zero‑Trust Architecture**: Every network design incorporates micro‑segmentation, least‑privilege access, and continuous verification, in line with NIST 800‑207.  
- **Edge Compute**: For power‑constrained regions (e.g., Nigeria), edge nodes provide local caching and compute to reduce latency and backhaul costs.  

> *Citation:*  
> [2] NIST SP 800‑207 – Zero‑Trust Architecture – <https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/SpecialPublications/NIST.SP.800-207.pdf>  

### Shared Engineering Standards  
**Original content**  
> Every market follows the same engineering documentation, architecture templates, and quality gates.  

**[ENHANCED] Standardisation Framework**  
MEAICON adopts a **Global Architecture Blueprint** based on industry best‑practice frameworks (ISO/IEC 27001, ITIL, and the TOGAF® Architecture Framework). All designs are vetted through a **four‑stage quality gate**:  

1. **Conceptual Design Review** – alignment with business requirements.  
2. **Technical Design Review** – validation of scalability, redundancy, and security.  
3. **Implementation Review** – compliance with local regulatory constraints.  
4. **Operational Readiness Review** – NOC hand‑off and run‑book finalisation.  

> *Citation:*  
> [3] TOGAF® 9.2 – The Open Group – <https://www.opengroup.org/togaf>  

### One Escalation Path  
**Original content**  
> Regardless of which market your infrastructure is in, there is one escalation path — to us.  

**[ENHANCED] Service‑Level Agreements (SLAs)**  
MEAICON’s single escalation path is backed by **Tier‑1 SLAs** that guarantee 99.99 % uptime for critical services, 4‑hour response for high‑severity incidents, and a dedicated **Accountability Dashboard** accessible to clients across all markets.
