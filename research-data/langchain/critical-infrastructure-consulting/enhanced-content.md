## Capabilities

### What we deliver

**Feasibility & Concept Development**
From greenfield site evaluation to concept design, we help clients assess viability before committing capital. We produce feasibility studies covering site selection, power availability, fibre connectivity, environmental constraints, regulatory requirements, and indicative cost models.

**Detailed Design**
Full engineering design for critical facilities — architectural, structural, MEP, electrical (including power distribution, UPS, generator systems), mechanical (cooling, HVAC, fire suppression), and network/security architecture. Design packages meet local regulatory submission requirements.

**MEP & Electrical Engineering**
Specialised MEP engineering for data centres and critical facilities — power system design (transformers, switchgear, UPS, generators), cooling architecture (CRAC, in-row, liquid-cooled), and physical infrastructure integration. We coordinate across trades to ensure system compatibility and performance.

**Construction Oversight**
Independent oversight of construction quality, programme, and compliance — from groundbreaking through commissioning. We conduct site inspections, witness testing, verify installation against design intent, and manage defect resolution with the construction team.

**Commissioning & Integration Acceptance**
Structured commissioning — Level 1 through Level 5 — verifying that systems perform individually and as integrated. We conduct integrated system tests (ISTS), blackout tests, and failover validation to confirm the facility meets design performance under real operational conditions.

**Operations & Standards**
Development of operations standards, SOPs, MOPs (Methods of Procedure), and runbooks for facility operations teams. We align with Uptime Institute operational sustainability standards and provide ongoing operational advisory to keep facilities performing to design intent.

**Vendor Qualification & Procurement Support**
Technical and commercial evaluation of vendors and suppliers — prequalification, RFP development, bid evaluation, and contract negotiation support. We independently qualify vendors and perform both technical and commercial evaluation to secure the right supplier at the right price and quality.

## Delivery Approach

### How it works

**01 — Engage & Scope**
We define the consulting scope, deliverables, and fee structure — with an explicit vendor-neutrality clause in every engagement. This phase produces a scope of work, deliverable schedule, and agreement on independence boundaries.

**02 — Assess & Analyse**
We assess the site, existing infrastructure, operational requirements, and constraints. Analysis includes power capacity, cooling loads, connectivity options, regulatory compliance, and cost modelling. This phase produces the engineering basis for all subsequent design decisions.

**03 — Design & Specify**
We develop detailed engineering designs, equipment specifications, and construction documentation. Design is reviewed with the client, architect, and contractor to ensure alignment, buildability, and budget compliance before procurement.

**04 — Oversee & Verify**
During construction and commissioning, we provide independent oversight — site inspections, witness testing, defect management, and final acceptance. Post-handover, we support operational setup, SOP development, and ongoing facility optimisation advisory.
