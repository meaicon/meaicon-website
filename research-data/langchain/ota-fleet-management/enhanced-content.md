# OTA Fleet Management

## MEAICON LLC FZ – OTA Fleet Management

**Positioning:** MEAICON LLC FZ delivers **OTA (Over‑The‑Air) Fleet Management** that securely updates, monitors, and maintains mobile data centre fleets across the MEA region.

## Why MEAICON

Managing thousands of distributed edge nodes requires a centralized, automated approach. MEAICON's OTA fleet management provides:

- **Automated Device Provisioning** – One‑click enrollment of new mobile data centre assets.
- **Remote Configuration** – Push‑based policy updates without physical access.
- **Health Monitoring** – Real‑time telemetry on power, cooling, and network connectivity.
- **Lifecycle Governance** – Automated decommissioning and retirement of obsolete equipment.

## Key Capabilities

| Capability | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| **Centralized Fleet Dashboard** | Unified view of all mobile data centre assets and their health. |
| **Secure OTA Updates** – Signed firmware/software updates with rollback capability. |
| **Proactive Health Checks** – Continuous monitoring of power, temperature, and network status. |
| **Compliance Reporting** – Automated generation of audit trails for regulatory bodies. |
| **Disaster Recovery Coordination** – Failover orchestration during site outages. |

## Benefits

- **Operational Efficiency** – Reduce manual inventory and provisioning overhead.
- **Rapid Deployment** – New sites can go live in days, not weeks.
- **Security Assurance** – All firmware/software is signed and verified before deployment.
- **Cost Savings** – Lower labor costs through automation and reduced on‑site visits.

## MEAICON Differentiation

While many OTA solutions focus on software updates, MEAICON integrates **fleet‑wide security posture management**—ensuring that every updated device meets the same Zero Trust and compliance standards as the rest of the infrastructure.
