## Overview

### Operational excellence, around the clock

MEAICON LLC FZ's managed services practice delivers the operational backbone that keeps critical infrastructure running. We combine Network Operations Centre (NOC) monitoring with Security Operations Centre (SOC) threat detection in a unified model, ensuring that performance anomalies and security events are correlated and triaged by a single team with full context.

Our approach is aligned with ITIL 4 for service management, ISO 27001 for information security, and SOC 2 for operational controls. We provide SLA-backed service levels with defined response times, escalation paths, and resolution targets tailored to the criticality of each managed system.

For MEA-region clients, we understand the operational realities — distributed infrastructure across multiple countries, varying telecom reliability, and the need for Arabic and English language support. Our NOC/SOC operates 24/7/365 with regional coverage that aligns with MEA business hours and on-call escalation.


## Capabilities

### What we deliver

**24/7 NOC Operations**
Continuous monitoring of network devices, servers, applications, and infrastructure components. We detect, triage, and resolve performance issues before they impact business operations. Our NOC operates with ITIL-aligned incident management processes including ticketing, escalation, and post-incident review.

**24/7 SOC Operations**
Security monitoring with SIEM, threat intelligence, and behavioural analytics. Our SOC analysts investigate alerts, hunt for threats, and coordinate incident response. We align with MITRE ATT&CK for threat classification and the NIST Incident Response lifecycle for structured incident handling.

**Infrastructure Monitoring**
Comprehensive monitoring across network, compute, storage, and application layers — using tools including Prometheus, Grafana, Datadog, and PRTG. We implement synthetic transactions, real user monitoring, and log aggregation for full-stack observability.

**Patch Management**
Structured vulnerability remediation — scanning, prioritisation, testing, deployment, and verification. We manage patching across operating systems, applications, and network devices with maintenance windows aligned to business schedules and rollback procedures for every change.

**Incident Response**
Structured incident response with defined RACI, escalation paths, and communication protocols. We conduct post-incident reviews (PIR) for all major incidents, producing root-cause analysis (RCA) documentation and preventive action plans.

**Change & Configuration Management**
Controlled change management with CAB (Change Advisory Board) processes, configuration baseline management, and compliance drift detection. All changes are documented, tested in staging, and deployed with rollback plans.


## Delivery Approach

### How it works

**01 — Baseline & Onboard**
We inventory managed systems, establish monitoring baselines, configure alerting thresholds, and document runbooks. The onboarding phase produces a Service Catalogue, SLA definitions, and operational documentation for each managed system.

**02 — Integrate & Automate**
We integrate monitoring tooling, SIEM platforms, ticketing systems, and automation runbooks. We implement automated remediation for common alerts — disk space, service restarts, certificate expiry — to reduce mean-time-to-resolution (MTTR).

**03 — Operate 24/7**
Continuous monitoring, alerting, and incident response across all managed systems. Our NOC/SOC team follows documented runbooks, escalation procedures, and communication protocols. Regular reporting provides visibility into availability, performance, and security posture.

**04 — Review & Improve**
Monthly service reviews, quarterly operational health checks, and annual SLA recalibration. We continuously refine alerting thresholds, automate additional remediation paths, and optimise the service catalogue based on operational data and client feedback.
