## Where maritime edge computing delivers

Maritime operators across the MEA region face scenarios where connectivity is satellite-dependent, environments are contested, and operational technology systems carry national security implications. MEAICON's platforms address three use cases that define modern maritime operations.

Vessel fleet tracking in satellite-dependent environments. Shipping operators managing fleets across the Gulf, Red Sea, and East African lanes cannot depend on continuous satellite connectivity. MEAICON deploys edge-compute nodes on every vessel that process position, navigation, and cargo telemetry locally — maintaining a real-time operational picture even during satellite outages lasting days. When connectivity is restored, buffered telemetry and integrity logs sync to the shore-side operations centre automatically, closing tracking gaps that previously lasted hours.

Vessel and port OT security. Cargo handling automation, ballast management, and navigation systems are operational technology environments with national security implications. MEAICON designs OT security platforms that provide platform integrity verification and remote control at every node — detecting anomalies, isolating compromised systems, and maintaining operational continuity. Post-quantum-ready cryptography protects telemetry links, ensuring that operational data remains confidential against future decryption capabilities.

Edge computing for offshore and remote operations. Offshore platforms and remote port facilities operate far from reliable grid power and network infrastructure. MEAICON's edge computing nodes operate grid-independent for weeks without resupply, processing sensor data, maintaining compliance records, and enforcing geo-fencing rules autonomously. When vessels cross into regulated maritime zones, the edge nodes automatically adjust data handling to comply with zone-specific sovereignty requirements.
