## Overview

### Resilience engineered into your infrastructure

MEAICON LLC FZ's disaster recovery practice is built on the principle that recovery capability must be designed, tested, and proven — not assumed. We work with clients to define Recovery Time Objectives (RTO) and Recovery Point Objectives (RPO) for every critical system, then architect the infrastructure and processes that meet those targets.

Our approach covers the full resilience spectrum — from high-availability design that prevents failures, to backup architecture that enables data recovery, to full disaster recovery sites that enable business continuity in the event of a primary site failure. We align with ISO 22301 for business continuity management and NIST SP 800-34 for contingency planning.

For MEA-region clients, we address regional risk factors — extreme weather events, power grid reliability variations, geopolitical instability, and the need for cross-border recovery sites. We design recovery architectures that account for these realities, not theoretical risk models from other regions.


## Capabilities

### What we deliver

**RTO/RPO Planning**
Business impact analysis to define recovery objectives for each system tier. We classify systems by criticality — mission-critical (RTO < 15 min, RPO < 5 min), business-critical (RTO < 4 hours, RPO < 1 hour), and standard (RTO < 24 hours, RPO < 4 hours) — and architect solutions to meet each tier.

**Automated Failover**
Infrastructure-as-code driven failover that automatically shifts workloads to recovery sites when primary site health checks fail. We implement orchestration with Ansible, Terraform, and cloud-native failover services, reducing RTO from hours to minutes.

**Resilient Backup Architecture**
Multi-tier backup strategy — 3-2-1 backup rule (3 copies, 2 media, 1 offsite), immutable backups for ransomware protection, and air-gapped backup repositories for critical data. We implement backup solutions including Veeam, Commvault, and cloud-native snapshot services.

**Disaster Recovery Sites**
Secondary processing sites — hot (active-active), warm (active-passive with automated failover), and cold (standby with data restore). We design DR site architectures that meet jurisdictional data sovereignty requirements while providing geographic separation from primary sites.

**Ransomware Recovery**
Recovery architectures specifically designed for ransomware scenarios — immutable backup storage, isolated recovery environments, and tested restore procedures. We implement zero-trust backup access to prevent backup compromise during an attack.

**DR Testing & Exercising**
Regular DR testing — tabletop exercises, component-level failover tests, and full-scale disaster simulation. We document test results, identify gaps, and remediate before a real disaster exposes them. Testing cadence aligns with regulatory requirements and risk appetite.


## Delivery Approach

### How it works

**01 — Assess & Classify**
We conduct business impact analysis (BIA), classify systems by criticality tier, and define RTO/RPO targets for each. This phase produces a recovery prioritisation matrix and gap analysis against current capabilities.

**02 — Architecture & Design**
We architect the DR solution — recovery site topology, failover automation, backup architecture, data replication strategy, and network connectivity between primary and DR sites. Design includes runbooks for manual failover as a fallback to automated processes.

**03 — Build & Test**
We implement the DR infrastructure, configure replication, and execute initial failover testing. Build includes documentation of recovery procedures, training of recovery teams, and validation of RTO/RPO targets through measured testing.

**04 — Maintain & Exercise**
Ongoing DR maintenance — replication health monitoring, runbook updates for infrastructure changes, and scheduled DR exercises. We provide quarterly test reports and annual DR plan recertification aligned with regulatory requirements.
