# We engineer the infrastructure businesses across MEA rely on.

MEAICON LLC FZ is a multidisciplinary technology group spanning connectivity, data centres, cyber security,
blockchain and consulting. We help enterprises and governments design the systems that need to work reliably,
securely and at scale — not just once, but for the long term.
