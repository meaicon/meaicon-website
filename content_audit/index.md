How an engagement runs

## Every build moves through the same four stages.

The sequence stays fixed. What changes is the depth we go to at each stage, set by your risk and budget.

01

### Assess

We audit existing network, facility, security and data assets against your operating requirements and regulatory context.

02

### Design

Architecture, vendor selection and budget are locked together, so the design you approve is the one that gets priced and built.

03

### Build

Engineering, procurement and commissioning run under a single project office with weekly SLA-bound checkpoints.

04

### Operate

24/7 NOC and SOC teams take over monitoring, patching and incident response once the system goes live.
